Phase 11 — Superposition Analysis

Question: Does the model encode more features than its 128-dimensional residual stream can orthogonally represent?

Method: Train sparse autoencoders at expansion ratios 1×–16× (128–2048 hidden features) on each residual-stream layer; track reconstruction R², active feature count, and dead-feature fraction.

Finding: R² exceeds 0.999 at 1× expansion for most layers — a same-size dictionary nearly perfectly reconstructs each layer's activations. No dead-feature spike and no plateau in active-feature count. This model does not exhibit classical superposition; its representations are already near-orthogonal within the 128-dimensional residual stream.

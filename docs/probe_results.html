Phase 4 — Linear Probing

Question: What information is linearly decodable from each residual-stream layer?

Method: Logistic and ridge regression probes trained on four targets — optimal distance (0–11), face solved (6 binary), corner orientation (8 binary), and scramble depth — applied at the embedding and after each of the four transformer blocks.

Finding: Optimal distance is 70% linearly decodable at the embedding and improves monotonically to 85% at the final layer. Corner orientation is perfectly encoded at the embedding (100%) but degrades across later layers, suggesting the model transforms it as distance encoding intensifies.
